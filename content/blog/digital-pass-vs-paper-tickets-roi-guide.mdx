## The Soft Costs: Labor & Gate Management

Physical tickets create a massive logistical bottleneck at the gate.
1.  **Search Labor:** Staff has to look up names on a printed list if a ticket is lost ($15-20/hr per staff member).
2.  **Replacement Work:** Manually writing new badges for "forgotten" tickets.
3.  **The "Line Death":** For every 1-minute delay at the door, guest satisfaction drops significantly. A happy guest spends more at concessions and is more likely to buy a ticket next year.

**EntryFlow Automation:** With instant QR check-in and local self-recovery of passes, your guest handles the "logistic" part themselves on their phone. Your staff only handles the "Welcome" part.
