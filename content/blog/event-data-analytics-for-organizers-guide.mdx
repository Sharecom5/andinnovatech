## 1. Monitor Registration Velocity
Are your registration spikes coming from Twitter, LinkedIn, or your email newsletter? 

**The EntryFlow Insight:** By using unique registration URLs for different channels, you can see live registration velocity in your dashboard. If you see a spike after a particular social post, you know exactly where to double down on your ad spend.

## 2. Real-Time Occupancy Tracking
Knowing how many people are in the room *right now* is critical for logistics, catering, and safety.

**EntryFlow Features:** Our dashboard shows you live check-in totals versus total registrations. 
*   **Peak Entry Times:** If you notice most guests arrived at 9:15 AM today, you can adjust your registration desk staffing for tomorrow or for your next event to match the demand.
*   **No-Show Analysis:** Identify the segment of your audience that registered but didn't attend, which is a prime group for a "Sorry we missed you" re-engagement campaign.

## 3. High-Fidelity Attendee Exports
The real value of an event is the relationships built. Those relationships need to be moved into your CRM as quickly as possible.

**One-Click CSV:** EntryFlow allows you to export your attendee list with a single click. 
*   **Checked-In Only:** Create a specific export of only the people who actually showed up. Send them a unique discount code for next year's early-bird tickets while the event is still fresh in their minds.
*   **Enriched Data:** Export contact details, registration times, and pass categories to segment your marketing list with high precision.
