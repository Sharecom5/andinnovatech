## What is EntryFlow?

EntryFlow is a specialized SaaS product that simplifies the entire lifecycle of an event pass. From the moment an attendee registers to the second they scan their QR code at your gate, EntryFlow ensures a seamless, branded, and secure experience.

Whether you are hosting a local workshop for 50 people or a tech conference for 5,000, EntryFlow scales with you.

---

## The 3 Pillars of EntryFlow

### 1. Instant Digital Pass Generation
Gone are the days of manually sending check-in details. The moment an attendee registers, EntryFlow generates a unique, mobile-optimized digital pass. Attendees can add it to their Apple/Google wallet or simply keep the secure URL handy on their phone.

### 2. The EntryFlow Verification Protocol
Security shouldn't be complicated. Our custom verification protocol uses cryptographic QR hashes to ensure that every pass is authentic. When your gate staff scans a code, our system checks it against our real-time database in less than 2 seconds, preventing any duplicate entries or fraud.

### 3. All-in-One Organizer Dashboard
As an organizer, you need data. The EntryFlow dashboard gives you a bird’s-eye view of your event’s health. Track registration velocity, monitor live check-in stats, and export your attendee data with a single click.
